Why MiCasaDirect

MiCasaDirect is where you book our Bucerías vacation homes directly with the people who own and look after them. No booking platform sits in between, so you pay less and you always know who you are dealing with.

10% off, every bookingThe direct-booking discount is applied automatically at checkout. There are no booking-platform service fees on top.
The price you see is the price you payThe total, including taxes, is shown before you enter a card. Nothing is added afterwards.
Live availabilityCalendars come straight from the system we manage the homes with, so the dates you see are the dates you can book.
Secure paymentYou pay by card on Stripe's secure payment page. We never see or store your card number.
Hosts who live hereJillian has lived in Bucerías for 13 years and has hosted guests for 11. She is an Airbnb Superhost with a 4.83 rating from 547 reviews.
A clear cancellation policyThe policy is shown on the checkout page before you pay and repeated in your confirmation email.

The Superhost status, rating and review count are from Jillian's host profile on Airbnb, as of November 2026.

How booking works

  • Choose your dates and pick a home.
  • Check the total, enter your details and pay by card.
  • We confirm the reservation and email you the confirmation, usually within a few minutes.
