    Tell us about your property

FAQ

Do I have to leave Airbnb?

No. Your Airbnb listing stays live and you keep taking bookings there.

Who receives guest payments?

You do, through your own Stripe account. MiCasaDirect does not collect or hold them.

What does it cost?

Plans start at $40 USD a month, with no MiCasaDirect booking commission. This is our introductory price; pricing and terms may change and will be confirmed before you sign up.

What do I need to get started?

An active Airbnb listing for the property and a Stripe account in your name or your business’s name.

When does it start?

The first group of hosts is planned for March 2027. Join the interest list and we will write to you before then.
